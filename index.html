New to Elevate?

Elevate is Illinois Tech's four-year career-readiness program. Every undergrad, in any major, gets hands-on experiences, an Elevate Success Coach and digital badges to show employers. You can start in your first year.

What this Hub does for you

  • Keeps the list currentAn agent re-checks IIT and outside programs, so new openings and deadline changes show up here.
  • Shows what you qualify forFiltered by your year, major and work status, so you don't waste time on programs that would turn you away.
  • Tells you what to do nextEvery program has a checklist with dates, from updating your resume to hitting submit.
